FAQ

Are my files safe?

Your STLs are never shared, posted or sold, and they're deleted after delivery. I can sign your NDA.

Can I use the images anywhere?

Yes: your store, Patreon, Kickstarter, ads and social media. I only show your renders in my portfolio if you say it's OK.

Supported or unsupported files?

Unsupported is best, so the renders show the model without supports. Pre-supported files work too.

What if I want changes?

One round of small changes (light, angle, crop) is included. A new scene counts as a custom hero scene.

How fast is delivery?

One model: within 24 hours, usually the same day. Big catalogs: we agree on a date before you pay.

How do I pay?

Upfront, per order. For big catalogs, half upfront and half on delivery.
